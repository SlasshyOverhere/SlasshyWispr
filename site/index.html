02 · SPECIFICATION

The surface.

REVISION 1.0.7 · 2026-05-09

WHAT IT IS

A precision instrument, not a chat app.

SlasshyWispr lives in your titlebar and disappears into your flow until you press the hotkey. From the moment you speak, every step of the pipeline (capture, transcription, language model, speech synthesis) is visible and tunable. Online, offline, or hybrid: choose per task, not per account.

/02 Language model ollama · openai-compatible · custom
/03 Speech synthesis piper · coqui · browser TTS
/04 Hotkey push-to-talk · toggle · wake phrase
/05 Wake phrase 'hey slasshy' · 'hey jarvis' · custom
/06 Pipeline visibility stt · ai · tts timings, live
/07 Credential store os keyring · dpapi fallback
/08 Auto-update background check · signed nsis

07 · STANCE

Offline by design.

NOT A FEATURE · A DEFAULT
/ POSITION

Your voice never leaves the device unless you tell it to.

Local STT, local AI, and local synthesis are first-class. The remote ones are opt-in per session, with a credential store that uses the OS keyring. The moment you take it online is the moment you ask.
